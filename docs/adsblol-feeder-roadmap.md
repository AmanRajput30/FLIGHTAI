# ADSB.lol Feeder Roadmap

Aervyn may later operate independent ADS-B receivers and feed data to ADSB.lol. 

## Feeder Compliance Rules
- **Authorization**: Do NOT automatically enroll or send receiver data without explicit authorization.
- **Copyright Waiver**: ADSB.lol indicates that contributors waive applicable copyright/related rights under CC0 in submitted feed data to the extent possible under law.
- **Proprietary Data**: Do NOT send proprietary, commercial, or non-authorized datasets into the feeder. Only feed data Aervyn is legally entitled to contribute via raw ADS-B reception.
- **Feeder Terms Review**: Before activation, Aervyn must formally review the current ADSB.lol feeder terms and conditions.
